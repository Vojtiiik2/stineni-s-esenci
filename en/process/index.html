How We Work

From the first measurement to final styling. Every step has its own pace, purpose and precision.

01 / 04

Consultation

Direction grows from observation.

We come to you, walk through the space on site and take precise measurements. We discuss whether you expect privacy, blackout or a purely decorative function from the window treatments. We show you fabric samples and recommend solutions that make sense in the space both technically and visually. After the consultation, you will have a clear idea of the available options and the direction the design will take.

02 / 04

Design & Pricing

Design turns into reality.

Based on the selected materials and technical solution, we prepare a quotation. This serves as the starting point. If the price needs adjusting, we look together for alternatives in materials, technique or the overall composition of the solution. The aim is to find the right balance between function, aesthetics and budget. Before production begins, you know exactly what will be delivered and at what price.

03 / 04

Production

Detail defines the result.

Once the quotation is approved and the deposit is paid, production begins. At this stage, the textiles are made and all technical elements are prepared. At the same time, we arrange the installation date so that it follows the completion of production smoothly. Production usually takes several weeks depending on the scope of the project and the selected materials.

04 / 04

Installation

Installation, finishing, calm.

We deliver the finished window treatments, install them professionally and hang the textiles with care. Both sheers and curtains are steamed, styled and adjusted so that everything works visually as well as technically. Once the work is finished, we tidy the space and hand it back ready to use. On site, we explain the operation and recommend suitable maintenance so the window treatments continue to function as they should over time.

Individual design

Every space has different light, different proportions and a different rhythm throughout the day.

The design takes shape directly on site through observing the light, the layout of the space and the way you use the interior.

For us, individual design means finding the right choice together.

20 years of experience

Experience gives us perspective and confidence when making decisions.

We help you navigate the options and bring function and aesthetics into balance.

The result is a solution that makes sense not only visually, but also functionally and in the long term.

Precision and refinement in detail

Quality of workmanship and technical execution determine whether the window treatments will continue to function well and look good even years later.

It is in the details that the difference becomes visible between a solution that only looks good and one that truly stands the test of time.

Precision and detail are visible at first glance. This is where your expectations meet our experience.

The result should feel natural

The goal is for you to feel naturally and genuinely well in the space.
